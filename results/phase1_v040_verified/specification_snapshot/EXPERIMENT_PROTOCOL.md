# Phase 1 experiment protocol
Status at definition: specified before the baseline results were observed. Research date: 2026-10-10. This implements the Phase 0 benchmark, not Phase 2 adaptive research.

## Frozen numerical experiment
The governing definition remains [MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATION.md](MATHEMATICAL_FOUNDATION.md) and [BASELINE_EXPERIMENT.md](BASELINE_EXPERIMENT.md): 120 by 80 mm L domain with 40 mm legs, thickness 5 mm, the two preserved pads, top-edge fixation, 20 mm distributed load patch, synthetic E=70,000 MPa and nu=.30, nominal force 1,000 N downward, density filter radius 6 mm, p=3, Emin/E=1e-6, raw minimum .001 and OC move .2. No projection or continuation. At most 300 evaluated designs, not 300 uncounted updates. Stop settings remain unchanged.

The primary volume fraction is .40. Two predeclared sensitivity settings, .35 and .45, use the same geometry, material, algorithm, 300-evaluation cap and prescribed load. They are **numerical settings**, not statistically independent geometry families. Each volume has its own equal-mass comparison; no pooled cross-mass ranking.

Candidates: B0 full solid (debug/reference, outside the mass budget); B1 exact-volume simple L-path; B2 constant-raw, filtered-density initialization; B3 final evaluated SIMP density; B3_binary ranked exact-volume reconstruction; B3_threshold conventional rho>=.5 reconstruction at actual volume. Both reconstruction rules preserve pads. A changed representation is a new candidate; do not repair disconnected geometry or hide material added by reconstruction.

For each frozen candidate, solve the Q4 model at h=2,1,.5 mm while inheriting each original cell's physical density. Do not refilter/reoptimize while refining. Independently assemble quadratic triangles on the same material interfaces at those resolutions, with separately constructed load and boundary data. The fine triangle model may require more memory than Q4. Invalid binary geometry is rejected before a solver call. B2 needs its coarse diagnostic solve; the primary independent audit targets B0, B1, B3 and the two reconstructions. Test a separately reoptimized h=1 B3 if the measured resource pilot permits; label it optimization mesh dependence, not fixed-design convergence.

The implementation uses an independently written P2 triangle assembler with NumPy/SciPy instead of adding scikit-fem. The Phase 0 proposal allowed another audited independent solver. Q4 and P2 share schemas and sparse linear algebra, not element shape functions or stiffness/traction assembly. This provides implementation diversity, not third-party certification; both need analytical checks.

## Numerical acceptance and status
Keep Phase 0 residual 1e-8, symmetry 1e-12, reaction/moment 1e-7, energy 1e-8 and volume excess 1e-6 times full volume gates. For fixed-design h1 to h.5, compare compliance at 1% and maximum pad displacement at 2%. Compare finest Q4/P2 with the same 1%/2% gates. Report coarse trends as well. A failed gate remains failed; refine or investigate as a separate recorded experiment instead of weakening it.

`solver_passed` means the discrete equations passed the declared algebraic checks. `independent_verified` additionally requires fixed-design refinement and independent-assembly agreement for the exact named representation. `optimization_feasible` checks only actual density bounds, pads, volume and equilibrium. Binary geometry checks require edge connection and preserved pads. No response safety/strength criterion exists. Physical validation and manufacturing feasibility are `not_assessed`.

Run the canonical optimizer twice and compare raw/physical arrays and numerical history after excluding times. Save exact arrays, content hashes, settings, code hashes, versions, solver counts, progress and stopping cause. Floating-point reproducibility is tested on this recorded machine; no cross-platform bit identity is presumed.

## Budget and selection comparisons
Phase 1 measures discrepancies and defines the selection comparison. It does **not implement an unproven learned selector**. SIMP is the candidate generator, not a competing selection policy. Allocation methods will receive the same frozen candidates and cheap information generated by that common process.

The preliminary equal-mass decision compares B1 and B3_binary within each volume setting. Report the coarse prediction, independent audit result, any reversal or abstention, and validity failures. Two alternatives do not establish a sampling-strategy advantage. Exhaustive reference auditing pays for all attempts. At checkpoints of one and two independent audit attempts, a fixed candidate-ID policy and coarse-objective ordering must admit only already-audited, numerically accepted candidates. A failed audit consumes its actual time and any solver calls, not an invented successful response. A complete numerical audit consists of its required mesh/solver sequence, not merely the cheapest P2 solve.

The larger H2 comparison remains gated by [RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md](RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md): expand a within-volume catalogue only after measuring useful discrepancy and costs. Required established baselines are deterministic coarse ranking, seeded random/space-filling order, expected verified improvement per estimated cost, and an R11-style finite-catalogue knowledge-gradient policy. U-learning/constrained expected improvement require a justified, frozen response threshold, absent in v0. The proposed strategy must specify its exact formula and distinguish it from those baselines before implementation.

All methods must pay the same initialization and mandatory final verification costs. Query costs used for decisions are prospective estimates from allowed data; hidden reference runtimes cannot guide a policy. Actual runtime is charged after querying. Offline oracle construction is reported separately. A complete catalogue optimum is a hindsight reference, never an algorithm available at a partial budget. Future stochastic pilots use seeds 0 through 9; confirmation needs variance-based sample sizing and held-out geometry/load families.

## Metrics fixed before observing results
- Number of geometrically valid, mass-compliant candidates and number that pass full independent numerical verification, with denominators and unresolved statuses.
- Best independently verified compliance within each mass and representation family; maximum displacement separately in mm.
- Coarse-to-independent discrepancy and reconstruction discrepancy, separately labeled.
- Pairwise rank changes and changes in the selected B1/B3_binary decision; ties within declared numerical uncertainty remain unresolved.
- False-feasible rate for actual implemented predicates (e.g. volume/geometry), if a corresponding cheap prediction is explicitly made; response/strength false-feasibility is **undefined**, not zero, without response limits.
- Factorizations, RHS solves, evaluated optimization designs, failed calls, wall/CPU time, peak process memory, assembly/verification overhead and stop reason.
- Dependence on the three predefined volume settings and on mesh resolution; no statistical generalization or superiority claim from these related cases.

## Decision gate
Proceed toward an expanded discrepancy catalogue only after code checks pass and some candidates have trustworthy fine-reference labels. Implement selective verification only if discrepancy is decision-relevant, fine labels are reliable, and actual cost leaves room for a selector to help. If geometry conversion fails or numerical error dominates, repair/understand that mechanism before learning. If ordinary verification is cheap enough or the decision is unchanged, retain the conventional workflow and report no demonstrated need for Phase 2.
