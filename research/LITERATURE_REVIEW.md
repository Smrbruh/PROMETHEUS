# PROMETHEUS literature review

Research cutoff and access date: **2026-10-10**. Status: targeted primary-source review, not an exhaustive systematic review or novelty certification.

## Decision-relevant finding

The broad PROMETHEUS combination is already well populated: robust structural optimization, constrained sequential search, learned manufacturing criteria, autonomous mechanical testing, and simulation-to-experiment discrepancy learning all have direct predecessors. A bracket remains a useful reproducible benchmark; its generation is not itself a research contribution. The strongest immediate use of the literature is to define strong comparators and exclude claims that merely rename established methods.

This review separates **published method**, **author-reported result**, and **PROMETHEUS inference**. None of the papers' speedups or physical results has been reproduced locally during Phase 0. Bibliographic details and claim/access records are in [the source ledger](sources/literature.json), with consolidated citations in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md). The more focused hypothesis search is recorded in [RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md](RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES.md).

## Search scope and evidence standard

Searches used original publisher pages, author/institution repositories, proceedings, arXiv and author code/data sites. Both seminal methods and explicit 2024–2026 records were sought. An arXiv deposit is labeled a preprint unless a journal/conference identity was separately verified. Repository availability is distinguished from a reproducibility study; a paper license is distinguished from a code or dataset license. Search-engine crawl dates were not used as publication dates.

The search log and access failures are in [literature_search.md](sources/literature_search.md). Abstract-only evidence supports method existence and scope, not implementation details, convergence claims beyond stated assumptions, or quantitative superiority. Paywalled/blocked articles were not silently treated as fully reviewed. Patent coverage belongs to the consolidated prior-art investigation, not this scientific-paper ledger.

## Comparison framework

The following tables are a technical synthesis. Scaling assessments and proposed benchmark uses are **engineering inferences**, not measured PROMETHEUS performance. Let (n) be unconstrained FE degrees of freedom, (d) design variables, (q) independent load cases, (s) uncertainty scenarios, (N) training observations, and T_FE the measured solve cost. Sparse assembly is approximately proportional to element count for fixed-order elements. Actual solve scaling depends on dimension, mesh, conditioning, ordering and preconditioning; assigning every FEA a universal (O(n^3)) cost would be misleading. Factorizations/preconditioners can be reused for multiple right-hand sides on unchanged stiffness.

### Established structural and optimization methods

| Direction and mathematical idea | Good use; principal failure modes | Cost and data assumptions | Manufacturing and useful benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Density/SIMP + OC/MMA.** Relax element occupancy to ρₑ ∈ [0, 1], interpolate stiffness with penalization, use adjoint sensitivities; OC handles a basic volume constraint, MMA general constraints. [L01](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s004190050248), [L02](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/efficient-topology-optimization-in-matlab-using-88-lines-of-code/), [L30](https://doi.org/10.1002/nme.1620240207) | Strong default for many-variable compliance optimization. Local minima, gray regions, checkerboards without proper regularization, conditioning near voids and sensitivity errors remain. | No training dataset. Each iteration needs state solves and response-dependent adjoints; compliance is self-adjoint for fixed loads and symmetric linear elasticity. A scalar objective gradient need not require (d) FE solves. | Density output needs thresholding/reconstruction and re-verification. MBB beam, cantilever and fixed-domain bracket are suitable. Keep filter radius fixed in physical units across meshes. |
| **Level-set.** Evolve the zero contour of an implicit function using shape derivatives. [L03](https://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~allaire/ajt-jcp.pdf) | Useful for explicit solid/void boundaries. Initialization, hole introduction, reinitialization and boundary discretization affect reachable solutions. | No offline data; repeated FE/adjoint solves plus boundary evolution or remeshing. | Sharp boundaries help geometry extraction but do not enforce tool access, minimum radii or AM process limits. Compare the same physical domain and FE accuracy as SIMP. |
| **ESO/BESO.** Remove or add elements using structural sensitivity rankings; stabilize and filter updates. [L04](https://doi.org/10.1016/0045-7949(93)90035-C), [L05](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168874X07000777) | Binary layouts are interpretable; BESO can recover material removed earlier. Aggressive updates, initialization and mesh-sensitive ranking can create poor or disconnected structures. | No learning dataset; FE per iteration and ranking/filter work. Modern sensitivity-based BESO is not a genetic population search merely because it is called evolutionary. | Length-scale filters are needed; binary pixels still require geometric cleanup. MBB/cantilever with matched material volume provide fair comparisons. |
| **Genetic/Pareto optimization.** Population variation and nondominated selection, e.g. NSGA-II. [L06](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/996017) | Useful for mixed/discrete choices and low-dimensional black-box designs; yields alternative tradeoffs. Feasibility sparsity and population costs are serious in voxel-scale topology. | Roughly population × generations × analysis cost; no pretrained data. NSGA-II sorting is (O(MP^2)) for (M) objectives and population (P), but FE usually dominates here. | Evaluate each candidate's process constraints explicitly. Test on a small CAD/truss parameterization; do not compare it to SIMP using radically different design spaces. |
| **FEA + reanalysis/ROM.** Solve equilibrium in a trial space; approximate repeated solutions using a reduced basis or reused analysis information. [L10](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0045782512002289) | Conventional solver acceleration is a strong comparator. ROM may fail after topology changes, near instability or outside its basis. | Basis construction needs high-fidelity solves; reduced systems have dimension r ≪ n. Assembly and residual evaluation may still scale with the full problem. Refresh using residual/error checks. | A fast incorrect forward model cannot validate manufacturing. Compare objective and gradient errors, total wall time and refresh cost on the same sequence of designs. |
| **Worst-case/robust optimization.** Minimize a maximum over an explicit uncertainty set; distinguish this from expected-value or probability-of-failure design. [L08](https://www.esaim-cocv.org/articles/cocv/pdf/2008/01/cocv0609.pdf), [L09](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0045782516318102) | Finds vulnerability to allowed perturbations. Misspecified uncertainty sets, conservative bounds and nonsmooth changing worst cases limit conclusions. | Analytic/eigenvalue/quadratic structure may avoid scenario enumeration; otherwise outer design and inner adversary costs multiply. No training necessary. | Does not cover unknown defects automatically. For linear low-rank load uncertainty, include an exact or densely checked load oracle before proposing learned counterexamples. |
| **Geometric robustness and AM filters.** Optimize projected eroded/intermediate/dilated realizations; apply build-direction supportability rules. [L07](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-010-0602-y), [L23](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-016-1522-2) | Addresses geometric tolerances or unsupported overhangs. Continuation and nonsmooth approximations can affect convergence. The 2011 paper has a 2022 correction notice requiring review before reproduction. | Multiple realizations increase FE work; filtering is typically cheaper than solving. No training dataset. | Explicitly process-dependent. These methods do not alone predict residual stresses, defects, surface finish, anisotropy, assembly or cost. Use geometric test patterns and load-bearing examples. |

A weak unvectorized classical implementation is an unfair performance target. [top99neo/top3D125](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/a-new-generation-99-line-matlab-code-for-compliance-topology-opti/) (L32) provides a newer efficiency reference alongside top88. [Sigmund's methodological critique](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/on-the-usefulness-of-non-gradient-approaches-in-topology-optimiza/) (L31) is relevant when a fashionable search method spends many more FE evaluations on a problem with available gradients. This does not exclude derivative-free methods where discrete choices or unreliable derivatives justify them.

### Learning, inverse design and experiment selection

| Direction and mathematical idea | Good use; principal failure modes | Cost/data assumptions | Manufacturing and benchmark implications |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Differentiable simulation / inverse design.** Differentiate the discrete equilibrium residual, usually by implicit/adjoint differentiation. [JAX-FEM, L11](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010465523001479) | Useful when constitutive laws or design parameters make manual sensitivities cumbersome. Automatic differentiation does not repair incorrect equations, unconverged states or nonsmooth contact/remeshing. | No training data needed. Forward/adjoint solves, derivative memory and compilation are real costs. GPU benefit must be measured on available hardware. | Still requires a manufacturing model and independent solver. Check gradients with directional finite differences and test FE outputs against reference solutions. |
| **Neural design representation.** Map coordinates to density with network weights as design variables. [TOuNN, L12](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-020-02748-4) | Smooth, flexible parameterization; distinct from predicting solutions from a training corpus. Network bias/local minima can restrict designs. | Per-instance weight optimization still calls FE. No supervised corpus required; architecture and optimizer tuning must be charged. | Mesh-independent parameterization is not mesh-independent physics or guaranteed minimum feature size. Compare fixed-budget compliance and extracted geometry with SIMP. |
| **Neural surrogates / GNNs.** Approximate response fields by learned maps or mesh message passing. [MeshGraphNets, L15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03409) | Useful for repeated similar analyses. Long-range equilibrium, unseen supports/materials, geometric extremes and accumulated rollout error are hazards. | Requires representative solver trajectories/fields. Graph evaluation scales with edges × layers/hidden width; training-data creation and memory may dominate. | Surrogate stress is not a verified stress bound. Use held-out geometry families and load regimes; benchmark field errors, failure classification and verified design quality. |
| **PINNs / physics-informed ML.** Fit fields with observation, boundary and PDE residual losses. [L13](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021999118307125), [L14](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/df438e5206f31600e6ae4af72f2725f1-Abstract.html) | Potential value in inverse identification with sparse measurements. Optimization imbalance/ill-conditioning can defeat a physically informed loss. | Collocation points may replace labels, not computation; many training steps and high-order derivatives can be expensive. | Loss minimization alone gives no fabrication/strength guarantee. Begin with analytic elasticity checks; replacing a small linear FE solve is not presently justified for PROMETHEUS. |
| **Diffusion / neural generative design.** Learn conditional topology distributions; guide sampling with response/feasibility predictors. [TopoDiff, L16](https://decode.mit.edu/projects/topodiff/), [latent diffusion, L17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05624) | Can generate diverse proposals quickly after training. Floating material, missed load paths and surrogate exploitation survive plausible-looking output. | Offline topology/FE labels and model training must be amortized across many tasks. TopoDiff's author site specifies 33,000 64×64 training images plus guidance-model data. | Connectivity is only one manufacturing criterion. Measure independent FE feasibility, novelty to training geometry, wall time and repair cost; GAN improvement does not establish superiority to optimized SIMP. |
| **Reinforcement learning.** Optimize a policy over sequential design edits/component placements. [SOgym, L28](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07288), [truss RL, L33](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbuil.2020.00059) | Natural for reusable sequential/discrete design tasks. Reward hacking, invalid actions and weak transfer can obscure engineering quality. | Training can require many simulator interactions; include those costs and seeds. SOgym's existence does not imply a policy beats classical optimization. | Environment constraints must correspond to actual process limits. Test held-out loads/domains and budget-matched classical methods before any RL investment. |
| **Constrained Bayesian optimization.** Model objective and feasibility; choose expensive evaluations by an acquisition function. [L20](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/gardner14.html) | Attractive for a small geometric parameter vector or finite candidate catalog. Kernel misspecification, high dimension and inaccurate feasibility probabilities are weaknesses. | Standard dense GP training scales roughly (O(N^3)) in observations; acquisition optimization also costs time. Feasible/infeasible labels come from actual checks. | Can include measured cost/geometry violations; cannot certify an unevaluated part. Compare random/space-filling sampling, deterministic ranking and informed acquisitions at equal total cost. |
| **Active reliability / UQ.** Learn a limit-state surface and estimate failure probability under a stated input distribution. [AK-MCS, L21](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167473011000038) | Reduces expensive evaluations near uncertain failure boundaries. Rare disconnected failure pockets and overconfident surrogates are failure modes. | Requires both a probability model and sample/evaluation budget; probability uncertainty and surrogate uncertainty must be distinguished. | Applies to loads, dimensions or material variability only if modeled. Use analytical limit-state tests and independently held-out structural perturbations. Worst-case robustness is a different estimand. |

### Physical feedback and manufacturing process models

| Direction | Established result and remaining gap | Cost, assumptions and validation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Calibration and model discrepancy.** Represent measurements as simulator output plus systematic discrepancy and observation noise. [Kennedy–O'Hagan, L22](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9868.00294) | This is established statistical modeling. PROMETHEUS must separate sensor noise, parameter error, numerical error and model-form error; a flexible discrepancy term can conceal wrong parameters. | Needs informative measurements and held-out prediction. Calibrate fixture compliance/materials with independent observations, then evaluate new brackets/loads; repeated calibration accuracy is not validation. |
| **Autonomous mechanical experimentation.** [BEAR, L24](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7148087/) and [simulation-assisted BEAR, L25](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8010472/) | Closed-loop AM/testing and BO already have physical demonstrations. The second work explicitly incorporates FE information and learned discrepancy into experiment selection. | Hardware, print/test time and specimen variance matter. These are author-run experiments, not evidence of cross-laboratory replication. A manually supervised first bracket test can test the principle without building a robot laboratory. |
| **Manufacturing-aware learned response.** [PATO, L27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04552) | A learned AM crack-risk field, differentiable optimization and physical checks already coexist in prior art. A new name for that combination is insufficient. | Process-specific training/validation is required; crossing machine, alloy, orientation or process settings can invalidate the predictor. Start with simpler measurable geometric constraints. |
| **Bracket simulation-to-test comparison.** [Okorie et al., L26](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/13/24/13218) | Published PLA compression tests establish relevant feasibility of a small bracket comparison. They do not validate a proposed metal bracket or arbitrary service loading. | Control printing, conditioning, fixtures and displacement measurement. Test repeated specimens; preserve failed specimens and deviations instead of reporting only a best-looking prototype. |

## Benchmarks and independent evidence

Use three distinct evidence levels:

1. **Numerical verification:** analytical patches/cantilevers and agreement of independent FE implementations establish whether equations are being solved correctly. A mesh study is necessary even when two codes agree.
2. **Algorithm comparison:** common domains, material laws, load/support patches, feature-size restrictions, evaluation budgets and stopping criteria establish whether a search method improves the same problem.
3. **Physical validation:** repeated measured specimens and held-out operating conditions test whether the chosen model predicts the relevant manufactured behavior.

SimJEB (L18) is a realistic geometry/meshing and surrogate benchmark, not a clean-room set of optimized bracket solutions or experimental truth. DeepJEB (L19) extends the available learning data, but synthetic descendants of source shapes must stay in the same train/test partition. Proposed PROMETHEUS split: hold out entire geometry ancestry/families and independently generated support/load configurations, rather than random nodes or nearly duplicated samples. This is a proposed evaluation rule, not a claim about an existing dataset's official split.

**Independent replication finding:** this review identified original code/data and later comparisons, but did not establish a controlled independent reproduction of each headline speedup or claimed improvement. Author cross-solver comparisons, follow-on papers by overlapping teams, public code and physical demonstration are different kinds of evidence. Recent diffusion, RL, nonlinear interpolation and learned manufacturing claims should therefore be treated as promising reported results, not validated universal advantages.

## Verified recent frontier and temporal limits

- **2024 preprints / 2025 publications:** DeepJEB and SOgym extend bracket learning data and reusable RL environments. Their final journal records were verified separately from their arXiv dates. [L19](https://dataset.narnia.ai/deepjeb.html), [L28](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095219762502281X)
- **2025 preprint:** physics-conditioned latent diffusion adds feasibility-oriented losses, but independent generalization and manufacturing evidence remain unverified here. [L17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05624)
- **2026 peer-reviewed result:** finite-strain interpolation consistency is still an active mechanics question; schemes equivalent under small-strain assumptions can diverge under large deformation. This reinforces the need to scope the initial benchmark to linear elasticity. [L29](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04332-8)

This is selective coverage, not a claim to have surveyed every 2026 publication. Recent active-learning and multifidelity robustness sources from the focused hypothesis search supplement this table in RESEARCH_HYPOTHESES and REFERENCES.

## Implications for the research question

The following are **PROMETHEUS research judgments** based on the evidence above:

| Proposed broad claim | Literature obstacle | Defensible next formulation |
|---|---|---|
| “An optimizer invents a lightweight bracket.” | Density, level-set, evolutionary and gradient methods already address this task. | Reproduce a transparent reference problem and identify a measurable failure of its validation pipeline. |
| “The optimizer discovers its own adversarial load.” | Robust worst-load optimization is established, including stress-related formulations. | Test a discrepancy source that a cheap exact load oracle cannot remove; beat that oracle where applicable. |
| “AI decides the next experiment.” | Constrained BO, active reliability and BEAR already do this. | Specify what information is selected, at what cost, for which decision error; compare strong acquisition rules. |
| “Learn from simulation-to-reality error.” | Statistical discrepancy modeling and simulation-assisted mechanical experimentation already exist. | Test a narrower audit/transfer assumption with held-out physical or higher-fidelity evidence. |
| “Combine manufacturing and physics-aware AI.” | AM filters, TOuNN, TopoDiff and PATO cover substantial parts of that combination. | Choose one manufacturing operation and one independently checkable feasibility criterion. |

A finite-catalog study of which reconstructed candidate deserves the next independent audit may have practical value, but neither finite catalogs, discrepancy models nor cost-aware sampling constitute novelty by themselves. The prerequisite is a reproducible discrepancy dataset that demonstrates an important decision error and an experiment capable of showing that strong established methods already solve it. If they do, retain the useful benchmark and reject the new-algorithm claim.

The initial non-AI baseline should remain authoritative: filtered SIMP/OC, modern classical implementation checks, independent remeshed FE verification and a declared load/geometry perturbation set. Learning earns a place only by improving verified outcomes after including data generation, tuning, repair and audit costs.
